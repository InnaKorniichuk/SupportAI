PASSWORD RESET

If a customer cannot log into their account because they forgot their password,
they should use the "Forgot password?" option on the login page.

The customer will receive a password reset email.

The password reset link is valid for 30 minutes.

If the customer does not receive the email, they should check their spam or junk folder.

If the email is still missing, the customer should contact customer support for further assistance.

Support agents should never ask customers to provide their password.
