REFUNDS

Customers can request a refund within 30 days of their purchase.

The support agent should check the customer's order and confirm that the purchase is eligible for a refund.

If the purchase is eligible, the refund request can be processed.

Refund processing may take several business days depending on the payment provider.

If a purchase is older than 30 days, the agent should explain that it is outside the
standard refund period and escalate the request if necessary.
