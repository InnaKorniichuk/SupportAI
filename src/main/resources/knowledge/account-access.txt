ACCOUNT ACCESS

If a customer enters an incorrect password multiple times, their account
may be temporarily locked for security reasons.

Customers should wait until the temporary lock expires before trying again.
They should not repeatedly attempt to log in during the lock period.

If the account remains locked or the customer cannot access it after waiting,
they should contact customer support.

Support agents must verify the customer's identity before making changes to account security settings.

Support agents must never ask customers to provide their current password,
password reset code, or full authentication credentials.
